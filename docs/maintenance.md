# Maintenance

The terraPen needs very little looking after. This page is deliberately short, because
prescribing a maintenance ritual the machine does not need would only waste your time.

## Cleaning

Use a **lint-free cloth** or **solvent-free wipes**.

- Wipe down the bed and frame whenever dust builds up.
- Keep the pen holder free of ink residue.
- Clear paper dust and stray fibres off the rails.

!!! warning "No solvents on the rails"
    Solvent-based cleaners can damage the rail surfaces and strip what little
    lubrication is there. Lint-free and dry, or barely damp, is all that is wanted.

## Lubrication

**Usually not needed.** Machines here have run many hours without any lubrication
becoming necessary, so treat this as a fix for a symptom rather than a routine.

If the carriage starts to feel rough or noisy as it moves, a **silicone grease with
PTFE** — something like
[Super Lube 92003](https://www.diy.com/departments/super-lube-92003-silicone-lubricating-grease-with-ptfe-3-oz-tube-translucent-white/0082353920037_BQ.prd)
— is the right sort of product.

Use very little, and wipe away the excess. Grease attracts paper dust, and too much
does more harm than none at all.

## Worth an occasional look

No schedule, just things to notice:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Belt** | A visual check for wear or fraying |
| **Screws** | Especially on the [toolhead](assembly.md), which you fitted yourself |
| **Wiring** | That the toolhead cable still runs cleanly and cannot snag |
| **Pens** | Cap them; a dried nib is the most common cause of poor lines |

## Between sessions

Cover the machine, or keep it somewhere it will not collect dust. Take the pen out if
it is going to sit for a while — see [transport and storage](transport.md).
